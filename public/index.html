Calendar age

Whole years, then months, then local calendar days in America/New_York. The remaining hours, minutes, and seconds are elapsed time. Each calendar step keeps your 3:02 PM birth time. Month lengths, leap days, and daylight saving changes come from the time zone’s calendar rules.

A fall-back day can leave 24 remainder hours before the next calendar day is complete.

Totals & decimal years

Elapsed totals subtract your birth instant from the displayed instant. A full day is 86,400 seconds. Decimal years = whole years + elapsed seconds since your last birthday ÷ seconds between that birthday and the next. Decimal displays marked ≈ are rounded; the exact whole-unit remainders and fraction appear underneath.

Where “now” comes from

The clock samples this site’s server automatically, estimates network transit, then advances using a monotonic browser timer. It resyncs every minute and when you return to the tab. The displayed timestamp is truncated to a whole second; every calculation uses that same second.

Sync details will appear here.

A note on precision

The arithmetic is exact for the shown timestamp. Live clock accuracy depends on the server clock and network delay, so this is not a certified time source. Standard JavaScript / Unix time does not count leap seconds. Your next birthday means May 21 at 3:02:00 PM Maryland time.
